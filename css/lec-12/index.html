Display:flex(parent property) justify-content

  • The justify-content property aligns the flexible container's items when the items do not use all avaliable space on the main-axis(horizontally)

  • The justify-content property can also be used on a grid container to align grid items in the inlline direction for pages in english,inline diraction is left to right and block direction is downward.

Display : Flex (Parent Property) Align-items

  • The CSS align-items property sets the align-self value on all direct children as a group. In flexbox, it controls the alignment of items on the cross axis. In grid layout, it controls the alignment of items on the block axis within their grid areas.
